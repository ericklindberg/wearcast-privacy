WearCast Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

App update availability

The improved location, account export, deletion, and recovery controls described below are included in a forthcoming WearCast update. If a control is missing from your installed app, contact wearcast.application@gmail.com. These controls do not change the fact that WearCast stores location and profile data on its server.

1. Information WearCast processes

  • Profile and preferences: a generated profile identifier, any name you provide, clothing and comfort preferences, routine, saved timezone, and reminder settings.
  • Location: your saved place name and latitude/longitude, obtained from location permission or a place you select. Device coordinates can be precise. Saved profile and trip locations are stored on the server and can be cached on your device. City-search text is sent to the geocoding provider.
  • Recommendations and feedback: forecast information, dated clothing recommendations, comfort ratings, feedback notes, any actual clothing choices you provide, and learned comfort settings. These records are associated with your WearCast profile.
  • Trip plans: destination coordinates and timezone, travel dates, trip preferences, forecasts, and generated packing information. Offline copies and packing edits may also be stored on your device.
  • Notifications: notification time and preference, device platform, push delivery token, and delivery records used to send and troubleshoot reminders.
  • Subscription records: Apple product and transaction identifiers, entitlement status, expiration or grace-period information, and subscription event records used to verify Plus access. Apple handles payment details for App Store purchases.
  • Access and service records: session credentials, a hashed recovery secret for supported accounts, request/feature usage counts, and operational delivery or error records. Usage counts are associated with a profile identifier; they are not automatically anonymous.

If you contact support, we also receive the information you include in your email.

2. How WearCast uses this information

WearCast uses this information to retrieve forecasts for the selected place, generate outfit and packing recommendations, adjust comfort preferences from feedback, deliver enabled reminders, restore access to the same profile, verify purchases, apply feature limits, and investigate service problems.

WearCast does not sell, trade, or transfer personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Service providers

  • Weather and place lookup: coordinates and relevant timezone are sent to Open-Meteo for forecasts. City-search queries are sent to its geocoding service. The US National Weather Service may receive coordinates when used as a fallback.
  • Notifications and app delivery: Expo and platform notification services process push tokens and notification content to deliver reminders. Expo also supports app updates. See Expo's privacy policy.
  • Apple purchases: Apple processes App Store payments and provides purchase/subscription information used to verify access. WearCast does not receive your full payment-card information through this purchase flow. Apple's handling of its records is governed by Apple's privacy policy.
  • Hosting: WearCast's backend is hosted on Render. Hosting and network providers process service requests and technical information needed to operate the service. See Render's privacy policy.

Provider records are governed by the provider's own practices. Deleting a WearCast account does not itself delete records held independently by those providers.

4. Storage, access, and retention

WearCast stores profile and related application records in its server database. The mobile app also caches profile, recommendation, and planning data so saved information can be available offline. Session and recovery credentials use the device's secure credential storage. Production app requests use HTTPS and authenticated endpoints for account data.

Saved profile, recommendation, feedback, learned-preference, usage, and trip records remain associated with your account until removed through the relevant control or account deletion. Signing out or uninstalling the app does not delete those server records.

Account deletion removes the active application records described below. It does not automatically erase previously created operational backups, support correspondence, or logs held by service providers. Specific backup and provider-log retention periods are not controlled by the app's deletion button. Contact support to request review of remaining copies and their deletion status.

5. Your choices and account controls

  • Location: select a place manually, correct the saved location, or manage device location permission. Turning permission off does not delete the saved location.
  • Notifications: change reminder settings in WearCast or disable notifications in device settings.
  • Export: in versions that include the control, Settings → Export my data provides the saved profile, recommendations, feedback, learned preferences, usage counts, and trip records. Credential secrets and push tokens are excluded from that export. The file can contain precise coordinates and personal notes.
  • Delete: in versions that include the control, Settings → Delete my account and data removes the active server profile, recommendations, feedback, learned settings, usage records, trips, notification registrations, and linked subscription state/event records. The app clears this device's local session and cached account data after deletion succeeds.
  • Recover: in supported versions, Settings → Show recovery code lets you save a private code for reconnecting the same account during setup on another installation. Anyone with the code can access that profile. Store it privately and do not email it to support.

Apple subscriptions must be managed separately. Account deletion, sign-out, and uninstalling WearCast do not cancel a subscription. Use Apple's subscription settings to cancel; see Apple's instructions. Restoring a purchase restores subscription access, not a deleted WearCast profile.

You may also email support to request access, correction, or deletion, or to ask about your data. We may need information sufficient to verify that the request concerns your profile. Do not send recovery codes, session tokens, or payment-card details.

6. Children's privacy

WearCast is not intended for use by children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Contact support if you believe such information has been provided.
